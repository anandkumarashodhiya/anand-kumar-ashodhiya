This portal brings together his work on Haryanvi Ragni tradition, Pingal Shastra, oral literature preservation, folk narrative systems, translation studies and digital archiving. It combines literary criticism, Pingal prosody, oral narrative analysis, vernacular poetics and archival practice, with the aim of placing regional literary traditions within modern scholarly frameworks. ## Explore - **Antaryātrā — The Inner Journey:** English transliteration, literary reflection and a preservation-oriented publication. - **Antaryatra Preservation Initiative:** a long-term archive for Haryanvi literary and oral traditions. - **Pingal Documentation Initiative:** preserving prosodic terminology and indigenous metrical systems. - **Triad Mission for Haryanvi:** a framework connecting preservation, scholarship and dissemination. - **Digital Folk Literature Archive:** structured repositories for regional literature. ## Preservation vision The central aim is the scholarly preservation of Haryanvi oral literary traditions through modern digital frameworks.
