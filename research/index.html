--- # 📖 Research Overview

This research ecosystem documents and preserves Haryanvi folk literary traditions through structured academic methodology integrating literary criticism, Pingal prosody, oral narrative analysis, vernacular poetics, translation studies, and digital archival systems.

The broader initiative seeks to establish regional literary traditions within modern scholarly, semantic, and globally discoverable research frameworks.

--- # 🏛 Preservation Vision

The central objective of this research initiative is the scholarly preservation and academic legitimization of Haryanvi oral literary traditions through modern digital frameworks.

The ecosystem integrates:

  • Prosodic analysis through Pingal methodology
  • Oral tradition documentation
  • Regional literary criticism
  • Translation and dissemination
  • Semantic indexing and metadata structuring
  • Digital preservation infrastructures

Particular emphasis is placed upon ensuring that regional literary systems remain discoverable, archivable, and academically accessible for future generations.
