📖 Research Overview

This research portal documents ongoing scholarly work related to Haryanvi Ragni tradition, Pingal Shastra, North Indian oral literature, folk-performance traditions, vernacular poetics, and cultural preservation.

The research ecosystem combines literary criticism, oral-performance studies, prosodic analysis, folk aesthetics, and digital archival documentation.

🏛 Preservation Mission

The primary mission of this research initiative is the scholarly preservation, documentation, and academic legitimization of Haryanvi oral traditions.

Through structured literary criticism, Pingal-based analysis, folk-cultural archiving, and digital dissemination, the project seeks to bridge indigenous oral heritage with modern academic frameworks.

Particular emphasis is placed upon:

  • Preservation of endangered folk traditions
  • Standardization of Haryanvi literary documentation
  • Prosodic analysis of oral poetic systems
  • Academic accessibility of regional literature
  • Global discoverability through metadata indexing
