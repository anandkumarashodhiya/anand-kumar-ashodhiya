# About the Researcher

The academic profile of **Anand Kumar Ashodhiya**: independent researcher, literary scholar, translator and folk-cultural documentarian.

![Anand Kumar Ashodhiya]({{ '/anand-kumar-ashodhiya-profile-photo.jpg' | relative_url }}){: width="240"}

## Academic identity

**Anand Kumar Ashodhiya** (आनन्द कुमार आशोधिया) is an Indian independent researcher, literary scholar, poet, translator and cultural documentarian. He specializes in **Haryanvi Ragni tradition**, **Pingal Shastra**, oral-performance studies, folk poetics and North Indian vernacular literary systems. He studied at Annamalai University.

His research combines traditional Indian literary knowledge systems with modern methods: folk criticism, oral-literature studies, cultural semiotics, prosodic analysis and archival documentation. His central concern is the scholarly preservation of Haryanvi oral traditions through research-oriented documentation.

| Domain | Specialization |

## Indian Air Force background

Before turning to full-time literary and research work, he served **32 years in the Indian Air Force** and retired as a **Warrant Officer** in 2016. Alongside operational duties, his service included administrative and human-resource functions. This experience shaped the archival discipline and documentation method visible in his scholarly work.

## Haryanvi Ragni scholarship

A major strand of his work is the systematic academic study of Haryanvi Ragni through literary criticism, folk-performance analysis and Pingal-based structural examination. It documents:

- traditional Ragni structures
- Saang-Shaili performance traditions
- prosodic systems within oral poetry
- regional folk aesthetics
- cultural memory and vernacular identity

His published work examines major folk narratives including **Heer–Ranjha**, **Kissa Bhagat Puranmal**, **Adhirājan** and **Draupadi: Ek Lok Chetna**.

## Pingal methodology and prosodic research

Classical Pingal studies focused on Sanskrit and medieval poetic systems. His methodology extends prosodic examination into living oral-performance traditions, to give Haryanvi Ragni literature a structured framework for technical analysis. The framework covers:

- Matra balance analysis
- Guru–Laghu sequencing
- Yati structures
- refrain systems
- oral rhythmic cadence
- Chhand-Vidhan documentation

## Translation and transcreation

He has also worked on translation and transcreation across Hindi, Haryanvi and English. His English-language works include:

- **SĀKET — An English Transcreation**
- **Niswarthi Udyoga Parva**
- **Kahaan Kahaan Paiband Lagau**
- **Antaryātrā — The Inner Journey**

These works aim to introduce regional literary consciousness to wider readerships while preserving the original cultural meaning.

## Folk preservation and cultural documentation

His independent initiatives include:

- Triad Mission for Haryanvi: From Boli to Bhasha
- Haryanvi Grammar Documentation Project
- Pingal Terminology Preservation Initiative
- Haryanvi–Hindi–English Dictionary Project
- Avikavani Folk Literature Archival Initiative

These are supported by GitHub-based digital infrastructure, metadata indexing, research repositories and ISBN-registered publications.

## Independent research status

He works as an **independent researcher** outside conventional university structures, and takes part in scholarly life through journal publications, digital indexing, research archiving and literary documentation. His research base is Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.
