# Research Mission

The philosophy and preservation-oriented mission behind the independent research of **Anand Kumar Ashodhiya**.

## Foundational vision

The research rests on the conviction that regional literary traditions are living repositories of cultural memory, ethical imagination, collective consciousness and indigenous knowledge. Haryanvi Ragni traditions, Pingal-based oral compositions, folk narratives and regional poetic structures are an intellectual inheritance that needs systematic preservation, scholarly interpretation and wide accessibility.

The mission seeks to bridge oral tradition and digital preservation, folk literature and academic scholarship, regional identity and global discoverability, traditional Pingal systems and modern research method, and cultural continuity and technological infrastructure.

## The Triad Mission

### Preservation

Documenting, archiving and safeguarding endangered Haryanvi oral traditions and regional literary heritage. This covers oral Ragni traditions, folk poetic structures, digital archives, indigenous literary systems and disappearing regional narratives. Many oral traditions are vulnerable to loss without systematic documentation.

### Scholarship

Applying Pingal methodology, literary criticism and interdisciplinary frameworks to regional folk literature. This covers Pingal Shastra analysis, prosodic and metrical studies, textual criticism, folk narrative interpretation, Lok Chetna studies, Mahabharata folk reinterpretation, and translation and transliteration research. The aim is to place Haryanvi Ragni within academically structured discourse while keeping its cultural authenticity and oral character.

### Dissemination

Making regional Indian literature accessible through digital platforms, metadata systems and scholarly discoverability. This covers digital publication, open access, metadata integration, indexing, citation identity and cross-cultural readership, so that regional traditions are visible beyond local circulation.

## Intellectual framework

The work draws on Indian literary traditions, oral heritage preservation, digital humanities methodology, folk epistemology, indigenous knowledge systems, metadata-based scholarship and independent interdisciplinary research. It treats regional literature as a significant repository of linguistic, ethical, poetic, historical and civilizational knowledge, not as cultural nostalgia.

## Preservation philosophy

Much of India's regional oral literature remains under-documented and underrepresented in mainstream institutions. The mission therefore goes beyond publication, to create permanent literary records, structured scholarly metadata, accessible digital repositories and documentation that lasts across generations, for the benefit of future researchers, literary historians, translators and readers.

## Long-term vision

A sustainable digital and scholarly ecosystem for Haryanvi Ragni preservation, Pingal scholarship, regional literary documentation, accessible folk research and independent knowledge preservation, so that India's regional literary traditions stay visible, accessible and meaningful for future generations.

> Preservation safeguards memory.
> Scholarship safeguards meaning.
> Dissemination safeguards continuity.
