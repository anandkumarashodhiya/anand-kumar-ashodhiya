# Research Interests

The principal scholarly domains, literary specializations, preservation initiatives and ongoing projects of **Anand Kumar Ashodhiya**.

## Academic orientation

His work focuses on the systematic documentation, preservation, analysis, translation and reinterpretation of Haryanvi Ragni literature within broader Indian literary and cultural frameworks. It integrates traditional Pingal prosody, oral literary preservation, folk epistemology, Lok Chetna studies, indigenous literary systems, comparative cultural interpretation, translation and transliteration methodology, and digital literary archiving.

## Primary research areas

**Haryanvi Ragni literature.** Study of traditional and contemporary Ragni as a structured literary and performative tradition, including its thematic, cultural and poetic dimensions.

**Pingal Shastra.** Technical analysis of the prosodic systems governing Ragni composition, metre, rhythm and oral poetic architecture.

**Oral tradition preservation.** Documentation of disappearing oral literary traditions through digital archiving, textual reconstruction and publication.

**Folk prosody studies.** Folk metrical systems and their relationship with regional literary aesthetics and performance.

**Mahabharata folk reinterpretation.** Reinterpretation of Mahabharata narratives through regional folk consciousness and Haryanvi Ragni.

**Lok Chetna studies.** Collective folk consciousness, ethical memory, cultural identity and social symbolism in regional literature.

**Folk narrative structures.** Narrative sequencing, dialogic composition, symbolic motifs and storytelling structures in oral folk traditions.

**Translation studies.** Translation and transliteration of Hindi and Haryanvi works into English while preserving poetic identity and cultural context.

**Indigenous literary systems.** Indian knowledge systems, regional literary methodologies and non-Western frameworks of textual interpretation.

## Current research projects

### Antaryātrā — The Inner Journey

A literary and transliteration initiative on the philosophical, poetic and introspective dimensions of modern Indian literary consciousness. It includes English transliteration and presentation, poetic preservation and archival formatting, digital publication with metadata, and cross-cultural accessibility.

### Antaryatra Preservation Initiative

A long-term effort to safeguard regional literary heritage, Haryanvi Ragni traditions, Pingal scholarship and folk poetic systems through structured digital documentation and publication. It aims to build literary archives, scholarly metadata systems, preservation repositories and bibliographic frameworks for future researchers.

## Methodological focus

Traditional Pingal analysis · textual criticism · oral history documentation · comparative literary interpretation · metadata-based scholarly organization · digital humanities approaches · archival preservation techniques

## Long-term academic vision

The aim is to contribute to the preservation of Haryanvi literary heritage and to build academically structured frameworks for indigenous literary systems that have been underrepresented in mainstream scholarship, bridging folk tradition, academic method, digital preservation and international discoverability.
