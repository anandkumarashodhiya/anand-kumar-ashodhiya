🏛 Research Mission

This page articulates the philosophical, scholarly, and preservation-oriented mission underlying the independent research ecosystem developed by Anand Kumar Ashodhiya.

📜 Foundational Vision

The research framework associated with Anand Kumar Ashodhiya is rooted in the conviction that regional literary traditions constitute living repositories of cultural memory, ethical imagination, collective consciousness, and indigenous knowledge systems.

Haryanvi Ragni traditions, Pingal-based oral compositions, folk narratives, and regional poetic structures represent an intellectual inheritance that requires systematic preservation, scholarly interpretation, and global accessibility.

This mission seeks to bridge:

  • Oral tradition and digital preservation
  • Folk literature and academic scholarship
  • Regional identity and global discoverability
  • Traditional Pingal systems and modern research methodology
  • Cultural continuity and technological infrastructure

🔺 The Triad Mission

The central research philosophy is organized around a three-fold mission framework:

🛡 Preservation

Documenting, archiving, and safeguarding endangered Haryanvi oral traditions and regional literary heritage.

This dimension focuses upon:

  • Preservation of oral Ragni traditions
  • Documentation of folk poetic structures
  • Digital archival development
  • Conservation of indigenous literary systems
  • Long-term cultural continuity
  • Protection of disappearing regional narratives

The preservation mission recognizes that many oral literary traditions remain vulnerable to disappearance in the absence of systematic documentation and accessible scholarly infrastructure.

📚 Scholarship

Applying Pingal methodology, literary criticism, and interdisciplinary research frameworks to regional folk literature.

This dimension includes:

  • Pingal Shastra analysis
  • Prosodic and metrical studies
  • Textual criticism
  • Folk narrative interpretation
  • Lok Chetna studies
  • Comparative literary methodology
  • Mahabharata folk reinterpretation
  • Translation and transliteration research

The scholarly mission aims to establish Haryanvi Ragni traditions within academically structured literary discourse while preserving their cultural authenticity and oral character.

🌍 Dissemination

Making regional Indian literature globally accessible through digital platforms, metadata systems, and scholarly discoverability.

This dimension emphasizes:

  • Digital publication infrastructure
  • Open scholarly accessibility
  • Metadata integration
  • International discoverability
  • Research indexing systems
  • Citation identity development
  • Global readership outreach
  • Cross-cultural literary engagement

The dissemination mission seeks to ensure that regional literary traditions are no longer confined to local circulation but become visible within broader global academic ecosystems.

🧠 Intellectual Framework

The broader intellectual orientation of this research initiative combines:

  • Indian literary traditions
  • Oral heritage preservation
  • Digital humanities methodology
  • Folk epistemology
  • Indigenous knowledge systems
  • Metadata-based scholarship
  • Archival sustainability
  • Independent interdisciplinary research

Rather than treating folk literature merely as cultural nostalgia, this framework approaches regional literary systems as intellectually significant repositories of linguistic, ethical, poetic, historical, and civilizational knowledge.

🌾 Preservation Philosophy

A substantial portion of India’s regional oral literature continues to remain under-documented, insufficiently archived, and academically underrepresented within mainstream literary institutions.

The mission of this research ecosystem is therefore not limited to publication alone. It seeks to create:

  • Permanent literary records
  • Structured scholarly metadata
  • Accessible digital repositories
  • Institutional-grade documentation systems
  • Intergenerational cultural continuity

The preservation effort ultimately serves future researchers, literary historians, translators, cultural archivists, and readers seeking access to indigenous literary traditions in academically organized formats.

🚀 Long-Term Vision

The long-term objective is to establish a sustainable digital and scholarly ecosystem dedicated to:

  • Haryanvi Ragni preservation
  • Pingal scholarship
  • Regional literary documentation
  • Folk research accessibility
  • Global metadata integration
  • Independent knowledge preservation

This mission aspires to ensure that regional literary traditions of India remain intellectually visible, academically accessible, digitally preserved, and culturally meaningful for future generations.

“Preservation safeguards memory.
Scholarship safeguards meaning.
Dissemination safeguards continuity.”

— Research Mission Framework
