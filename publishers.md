# Avikavani Publishers

![Avikavani Publishers banner]({{ '/avikavani-front-banner.jpg' | relative_url }})

## Our identity

![Avikavani Publishers logo]({{ '/avikavani-logo.jpg' | relative_url }})

Avikavani Publishers is a self-publishing imprint established to bridge India's oral folk heritage and modern scholarly expression.

## Our mission

Guided by military discipline and literary responsibility, our mission is the documentation and preservation of **Haryanvi folk literature** and the reinterpretation of history through cultural consciousness.

## Publishing philosophy

- **Textual authenticity:** maintaining linguistic integrity in Hindi and Haryanvi.
- **Scholarly depth:** annotations and *Pingal Samiksha* (prosody review) for folk epics.
- **Documentation:** long-term cultural preservation over commercial volume.

## About the founder

**Anand Kumar Ashodhiya** served 32 years in the Indian Air Force. A recipient of the **Haryanvi Sahitya Ratna (2025)**, he founded Avikavani to keep classical Indian voices vibrant in the digital era.
