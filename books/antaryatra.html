📖 About the Book

Antaryātrā (The Inner Journey) is a contemplative poetic work that delves into the depths of human consciousness, emotion, and philosophical reflection. Through a modern literary voice, the book captures the essence of inner exploration and self-realisation, making it a significant contribution to contemporary Indian English poetry.
