📖 About the Book

Prem Ke Sau Rang is a collection of modern Hindi short poems that explores the diverse emotional and experiential dimensions of love. Through concise poetic expression and evocative imagery, the work reflects on relationships, sensitivity, and the nuanced textures of human connection within a contemporary literary framework.
