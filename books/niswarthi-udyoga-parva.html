📖 About the Book

This work presents an English translation of a classical Indian narrative, bringing traditional themes of dharma, selflessness, and ethical responsibility into a contemporary literary context. The translation seeks to preserve the philosophical depth and narrative integrity of the source while making it accessible to a global readership.
