📖 About the Book

Draupadi: Ek Lok Chetna presents a critical reinterpretation of the character of Draupadi within the Haryanvi Ragni tradition. The work foregrounds the concept of lok chetna (folk consciousness) and situates it within a broader cultural and literary discourse. Through the integration of Pingal-based structural analysis, the text bridges oral tradition and formal literary criticism.
