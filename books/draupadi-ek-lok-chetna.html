📖 About the Book

Draupadi: Ek Lok Chetna reinterprets the character of Draupadi through the lens of Haryanvi Ragni tradition. The work foregrounds lok chetna (folk consciousness) while integrating literary criticism, cultural discourse, and Pingal-based structural analysis within a regional poetic framework.
