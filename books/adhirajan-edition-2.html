📖 About the Book

Adhirājan (Edition II) presents a revised and enriched version of the original Haryanvi folk epic. The work deepens its engagement with Pingal prosody and expands the literary articulation of the Ragni tradition within a broader cultural and analytical framework.

For detailed analytical studies based on this work, see: Adharajan (Ragini 1–8) and Adharajan (Ragini 9–16).
