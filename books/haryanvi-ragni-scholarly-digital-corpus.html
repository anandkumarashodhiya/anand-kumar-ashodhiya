📖 About the Book

HARYANVI RAGNI SCHOLARLY DIGITAL CORPUS (HRSDC) is a bilingual scholarly anthology documenting Haryanvi Ragni as a living tradition of oral literature, vernacular poetry and cultural expression.

Bringing together traditional heritage and contemporary voices, the corpus presents original Haryanvi Ragni texts with literary English translations, critical commentary, Pingal and poetic observations, selected language notes, and scholarly assessments.

Designed for researchers, scholars, students and readers of South Asian oral and vernacular literature, HRSDC brings an important regional literary tradition to the wider English-reading world while preserving the linguistic and cultural character of its Haryanvi source texts.
