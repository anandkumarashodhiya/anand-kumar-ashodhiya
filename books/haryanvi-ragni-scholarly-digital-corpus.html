📖 About the Book

HARYANVI RAGNI SCHOLARLY DIGITAL CORPUS (HRSDC) is a bilingual scholarly anthology documenting Haryanvi Ragni as a living tradition of oral literature, vernacular poetry and cultural expression.

Bringing together traditional heritage and contemporary voices, the corpus preserves original Haryanvi Ragni texts alongside literary English translations, critical commentary, Pingal metric observations, selected language notes, and scholarly assessments.

Designed for researchers, scholars, students, and readers of South Asian oral and vernacular literature, HRSDC seeks to make an important regional literary tradition accessible to a wider global audience while preserving the authentic linguistic texture of its source compositions.
