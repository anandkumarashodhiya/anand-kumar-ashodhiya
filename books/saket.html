📖 About the Book

SĀKET is an English transcreation that reinterprets the classical Indian epic tradition through a process of literary transformation rather than literal translation. The work preserves the aesthetic and philosophical depth of the source tradition while rendering it accessible to a contemporary global readership. By negotiating between cultural fidelity and creative re-expression, it contributes to the evolving discourse on translation, transcreation, and cross-cultural literary adaptation.
