📖 About the Book

This work presents a Haryanvi Ragni-based literary rendering of the classical Heer–Ranjha narrative, integrating oral folk tradition with structured poetic expression grounded in Pingal prosody. Beyond literary preservation, the text engages with cultural symbolism, emotional archetypes, and narrative continuity within the North Indian folk imagination.

🔬 Research Integration

This book forms the primary textual foundation for a structured academic research series analyzing the Heer–Ranjha narrative within the Haryanvi Ragni tradition. The following peer-reviewed studies examine its thematic, cultural, and prosodic dimensions:

  • Heer–Ranjha (Ragnis 1–7) — narrative formation and thematic foundations
  • Heer–Ranjha (Ragnis 8–16) — viraha, social structure, and transformation
  • Heer–Ranjha (Ragnis 23–28) — culmination, tragedy, and cultural memory
