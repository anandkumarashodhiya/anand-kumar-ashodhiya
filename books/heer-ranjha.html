📖 About the Book

Heer Ranjha presents a Haryanvi Ragni-based literary adaptation of the celebrated love legend, combining folk poetic expression with analytical insights grounded in Pingal prosody. The work contributes to both literary preservation and scholarly interpretation of oral traditions.

For a detailed analytical study based on this work, see the research articles: Heer–Ranjha (Ragnis 1–7), Heer–Ranjha (Ragnis 8–16), and Heer–Ranjha (Ragnis 23–28).
