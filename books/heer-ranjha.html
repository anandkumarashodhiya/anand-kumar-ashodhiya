Heer Ranjha — Haryanvi Ragni Collection

📖 About the Book

This work presents a Haryanvi Ragni-based literary rendering of the celebrated Heer–Ranjha narrative, combining oral folk expression with structural awareness of Pingal prosody. It serves as a foundational text for a series of analytical research studies examining the Ragni tradition in a scholarly framework.
