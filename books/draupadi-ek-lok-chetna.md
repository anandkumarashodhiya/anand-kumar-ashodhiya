## 📖 About the Book

Draupadi: Ek Lok Chetna presents a critical reinterpretation of the character of Draupadi within the Haryanvi Ragni tradition. The work foregrounds the concept of **lok chetna** (folk consciousness) and situates it within a broader cultural and literary discourse.
