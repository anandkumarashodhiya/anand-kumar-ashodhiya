📖 About the Book

Avikavani Haryanvi Ragni Sangrah presents a curated and structured collection of Haryanvi ragnis, documenting the richness of oral folk traditions within a stable literary framework. The volume contributes to the preservation of regional poetic heritage while reflecting an intrinsic awareness of metrical organisation and cultural expression rooted in traditional performance practices.
