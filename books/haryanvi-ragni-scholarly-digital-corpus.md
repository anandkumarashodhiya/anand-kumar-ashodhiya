## About the Book

**HARYANVI RAGNI SCHOLARLY DIGITAL CORPUS (HRSDC)** is a comprehensive bilingual scholarly anthology documenting Haryanvi Ragni as a vibrant tradition of oral literature, vernacular poetry, and cultural performance.

Bringing together traditional heritage and contemporary voices, the corpus presents original Haryanvi Ragni texts alongside literary English translations, critical commentary, Pingal metric observations, selected language notes, and scholarly assessments.

Designed for researchers, scholars, students, and readers of South Asian oral and vernacular literature, HRSDC situates this regional poetic tradition within global literary discourse while rigorously preserving the linguistic and cultural authenticity of its source texts.
