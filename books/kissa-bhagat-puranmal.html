📖 About the Book

This work presents a devotional and culturally significant narrative within the Haryanvi Ragni tradition, combining oral storytelling with structural awareness of Pingal prosody. It functions both as a literary text and as a foundational source for analytical and interpretive research on folk poetic traditions.

📚 Academic Context

This book forms part of an ongoing scholarly research framework examining Haryanvi Ragni literature through the lens of Pingal Shastra and cultural poetics.
