📖 About the Book

Kissa Bhagat Puranmal presents a devotional and culturally significant narrative through the Haryanvi Ragni tradition. The work integrates oral storytelling with analytical insights rooted in Pingal prosody, offering both literary richness and scholarly depth.

For a detailed analytical study based on this work, see the research articles: Bhagat Puranmal (Ragni 1–8) and Bhagat Puranmal (Ragni 9–18).
