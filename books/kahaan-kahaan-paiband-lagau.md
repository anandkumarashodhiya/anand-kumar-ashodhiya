## About the Book

**Kahaan Kahaan Paiband Lagau** is a powerful English translation of Saroj Dahiya’s celebrated Hindi novel—an intimate, unflinching exploration of a woman’s endurance within the delicate and often unforgiving structures of North Indian domestic life. 

Through the twelve-year journey of Sujata, caught between marital neglect and the subtle emotional burdens of her parental home, the narrative depicts how ordinary women navigate fractures in their lives, stitching them together one painful *“paiband”* (patch) at a time. Facing poverty, pregnancy, societal gossip, and loneliness, Sujata steadfastly upholds her dignity against persistent adversities.

### Linguistic Sensitivity and Cultural Authenticity
Translated by Anand Kumar Ashodhiya, this English edition renders the novel’s psychological depth and emotional weight into lucid prose while carefully safeguarding its rural Haryanvi idioms, speech rhythms, and socio-cultural nuances. 

### Critical Apparatus of This Edition
To facilitate engagement for international readers and academic researchers, this edition features:
- **Translator’s Note:** Insights into socio-linguistic context and translation methodology.
- **Academic Apparatus:** Foreword, Preface, and Scholarly Review analyzing the socio-cultural dynamics of rural domesticity.
- **Literary–Critical Afterword:** A study of Saroj Dahiya’s narrative structure and voice.
- **Comprehensive Glossary:** Detailed explanations of regional kinship terms, cultural rituals, and folk idioms.

> *“A timeless narrative of survival, dignity, and the quiet courage of women who continue to patch their lives where the world keeps tearing them.”*
