Kahaan Kahaan Paiband Lagau presents an English poetic translation that captures the emotional intensity and social consciousness of its source text. Through a nuanced process of literary reconstruction, the work reflects fragmented human experiences, socio-cultural tensions, and the continuity of expression across linguistic boundaries.
