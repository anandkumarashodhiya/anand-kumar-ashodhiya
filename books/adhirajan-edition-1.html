📖 About the Book

Adhirājan (Edition I) represents a foundational contribution to the Haryanvi Ragni tradition, grounded in Pingal prosody and the oral performance aesthetics of North Indian folk literature. The work reflects a synthesis of traditional narrative structures with metrical discipline, offering a literary framework that supports both performative and analytical interpretation.
