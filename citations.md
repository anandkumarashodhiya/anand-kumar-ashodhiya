## 📚 Research Domains

The scholarly contributions primarily engage with:

- Pingal Shastra (Prosody & Chhand Analysis)  
- Haryanvi Ragni Tradition  
- Indian Oral & Folk Literature  
- Cultural and Textual Interpretation  

---

## 🧭 Academic Contribution

The research establishes a **structured Pingal-based analytical framework** for the study of Haryanvi Ragni literature. It contributes toward bridging **oral folk traditions with formal literary criticism**, enabling systematic scholarly engagement within the broader field of Indian literary studies.
