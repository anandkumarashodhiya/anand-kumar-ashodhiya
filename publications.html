📄 Research Publications

Anand Kumar Ashodhiya
Independent Researcher | Former Warrant Officer, Indian Air Force

This publication archive presents the peer-reviewed scholarly corpus of Anand Kumar Ashodhiya devoted to Haryanvi Ragni literature, Pingal Shastra, Saang-Shaili traditions, vernacular poetics, oral-performance aesthetics, folk philosophy, ethical folk consciousness, and North Indian oral literary systems. The publications collectively examine prosody, performative rhythm, Chhand architecture, Guru-Laghu sequencing, Yati structure, Bhakti aesthetics, folk hermeneutics, cultural memory, and oral epistemology through interdisciplinary literary methodologies.
