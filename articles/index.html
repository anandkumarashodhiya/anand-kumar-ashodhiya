📄 Research & Scholarly Articles

This curated research hub presents a structured corpus of peer-reviewed scholarly articles by Anand Kumar Ashodhiya, focusing on Haryanvi Ragni tradition, Pingal Shastra (Indian prosody), and North Indian folk literary systems.

These studies collectively contribute to an ongoing academic research programme examining prosodic structures, narrative aesthetics, and socio-cultural frameworks within oral performance traditions.
