# Adharajan ki Haryanvi Saang-Shaili Raginian: Pingal Shastra evam Lok-Sanskritik Pariprekshya mein ek Vishleshanatmak Adhyayan (Ragini 1–8 ke Sandarbh mein)

**Author:** Anand Kumar Ashodhiya (कवि आनन्द शाहपुर)

**Summary:** This study examines the opening raginis (1–8) of the Adharajan narrative through Pingal prosody and Haryanvi folk-poetic traditions. It explores metrical structure, narrative aesthetics, and socio-cultural symbolism within Saang performance frameworks.

The study is based on the book [Adhirājan (Edition II) — Haryanvi Folk Epic]({{ page.citation.book | relative_url }}).

## Abstract

This research paper presents an in-depth analysis of the Haryanvi Saang-style raginis (Ragini 1–8) from the narrative series *Adharajan*, examined through literary, prosodic, and socio-cultural perspectives. Rooted in the rich oral performance aesthetics of Haryana and the historical milieu of 19th-century Jaipur, the study scans the metric patterns under classical Pingal Shastra, illuminating how vernacular poets synthesized rigid prosodic disciplines with spontaneous oral transmission.

## Scholarly Context

This article is part of a broader research series on Haryanvi Ragni literature, examined through the framework of Pingal Shastra and folk-cultural analysis. Complementary studies by the author on *Heer–Ranjha* and *Bhagat Puranmal* offer comparative insights into prosody, narrative progression, and gendered voice in North Indian folk traditions.
