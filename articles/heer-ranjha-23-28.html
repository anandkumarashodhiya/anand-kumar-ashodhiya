Abstract

This paper explores the integration of Pingal Shastra within the Haryanvi Ragni tradition, focusing on the cultural and poetic analysis of Heer–Ranjha narratives (Ragnis 23–28). It highlights how prosodic discipline enhances lyrical expression and preserves oral traditions.
