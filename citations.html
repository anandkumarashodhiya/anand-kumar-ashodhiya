🧭 Academic Contribution

The research work contributes toward the systematic documentation and analytical interpretation of Haryanvi Ragni literature through a Pingal-based framework, thereby bridging traditional oral forms with formal literary criticism. These studies aim to establish a structured methodology for examining regional poetic traditions within a broader Indian literary discourse.
