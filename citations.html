--- ## 📚 Research Domains The scholarly contributions primarily engage with the following areas:
  • Pingal Shastra (Prosody and Chhand Analysis)

--- ## 🧭 Academic Contribution The research work contributes toward:

The systematic documentation and analytical interpretation of Haryanvi Ragni literature through a Pingal-based framework, thereby bridging traditional oral forms with formal literary criticism. These studies aim to establish a structured methodology for examining regional poetic traditions within a broader Indian literary discourse.
