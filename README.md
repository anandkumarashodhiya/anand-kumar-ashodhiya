## 👤 About the Author

**Anand Kumar Ashodhiya**  
Independent Researcher | Former Warrant Officer, Indian Air Force  

A multidisciplinary literary scholar working across **Hindi, English, and Haryanvi literature**, with a focus on:

- Folk epics and oral traditions  
- Pingal Shastra (prosody and metrics)  
- Cultural and literary analysis  
- Translation and transcreation  

🌐 **Official Website:**  
https://anandkumarashodhiya.github.io/anand-kumar-ashodhiya/

## 📖 Research Focus

This repository is dedicated to the systematic study and documentation of:

- **Haryanvi Ragni Tradition**
- **Heer–Ranjha Folk Narrative (Analytical Series)**
- **Pingal Shastra and Chhand Vidhan**
- **Indic Folk Literature and Cultural Studies**

## 🧠 Major Research Projects

### 🔹 Heer–Ranjha Ragni Analysis Series

A multi-part academic study examining the narrative, cultural, and metrical dimensions of the Heer–Ranjha tradition in Haryanvi ragni form.

---

### 🔹 Adhrajan Project (Grand Review – Ongoing)

A comprehensive literary and cultural analysis project intended for international publication as a consolidated scholarly work.

---

### 🔹 Antaryatra Project

A long-term literary initiative focused on:

- Compilation  
- Translation  
- Publication
